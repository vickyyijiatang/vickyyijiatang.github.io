About

Vicky
Yijia Tang
唐藝嘉

Vicky Yijia Tang (唐藝嘉) is an artist and movement researcher undertaking a practice-as-research PhD at UAL, working with improvisation, drawing, and embodied movement through a methodology she describes as Improvised Moving–Drawing (IMD).

Trained in life drawing, gestural mark-making, and calligraphy, and in movement practices including Tai Chi, Authentic Movement, and Skinner Releasing Technique, she works at the threshold between visual art and somatic practice.

The research investigates how the material qualities of mark-making register affective embodied experience at an infra-linguistic level — what the body inscribes before language arrives.

回国三年 / Three Years in China

Film · Drawing · Performance · Wuhan · Shanghai · Shenzhen · 2020–2023

浮生若梦

晃动 起伏   稀疏抖动的鼓点   捉住 抽离
坠落 荒野 游魂     暗影 浮动   身之船只
飘动的液体   抖动的眼睛   暗涌之下 苍穹之上
飘过表皮的思绪   重叠的内外   空谷回声   火之欲   流动的花影
空城舞魂   爬上你的窗   捕捉阳光的人

我来到城市的边缘，放慢这趟凯旋式奔跑的速度，改成了稳重的行走。整个地区飘浮着懒洋洋和放荡的罪恶气息；房屋、店铺、人流，有时候似乎只是它发烧的身体的一阵哆嗦，热病引起的昏梦导致的鸡皮疙瘩。

当城市的红晕渐渐暗淡成紫色时，整个世界骤然荒凉、枯萎起来……暗涌 浮生

孤独中狂欢，在城市中游离失所，在奔跑中失去，在梦境中醒来，飘动中迷失，迷失于失去的城市。眼中的雾又再起，在流动中迷失，在流动中寻找自我，奔跑流走中寻找方向。浮动的光影，美丽又灿烂，在城市的运动中找到一种动力。

Big carnival, we were lost inside, alone around the crowd of the city. We're running, waking up and floating in our dreams. Mist filled up in my eyes, I couldn't see anything. So I keep running, moving to find my direction. I see the light of the shadow, shining openly. There exists energy which drives you to keep going. Came across familiar strangers, the river was like the traffic, universal neon lights, they just moved endlessly. The leaves shake the bodies with the flower. People die inside the sea while that carries their spirit, come and go with the flow. You see the fishbone dancing under the railway. Flower flowing.

Summer in New York

Illustration · Performance · SVA New York

The first time I went to New York, it was summer. Loud, hot, alive.

I was on an illustration residency at SVA. I worked with professional dancers for the first time. Things I thought were impossible started happening.

My knee was dislocated. I danced Gaga anyway.

The colours I made that summer are bright and a little wild. Looking back at that version of myself, I see a child — carrying so much pain inside, but building a paradise in the New York summer heat.

流浪汉项目 / The Eyes Fill Up with Water

Performance · Exhibition · Arlington Hostel, Camden · London · RCA

London holds a particular kind of grief — people who have left home, people for whom home is in motion, people from everywhere and nowhere at once.

This project began with six months of volunteering at Arlington Hostel in Camden, working alongside homeless residents at The Creative Space — a studio run by SPACE for people without homes. Together we made masks, danced, drew, performed.

The resulting work, The Eyes Fill Up with Water, places two moving image pieces facing each other — like a book holding memories. Mask-making workshops translated communication into performance. I separated myself into 'double motions', travelling between myself and four masked dancers in a kind of call and response.

We broke the silence. We made something beautiful together.

Body Archive

Performance · Tate Britain Archive Room · RCA · 2018

The archive holds objects. Documents. Things that stay.

What happens when a body enters?

Performed in the archive room at Tate Britain, this work placed the living body among stored objects — not as a visitor, but as a participant. The archive is the past we have created. The body is alive. When a living body moves through its own archive, it is moving through its own memory.

What does the body leave behind? What does it carry forward?

民族舞 / National Identity

Illustration · Collage · Pre-RCA

China's cultural propaganda presents a seemingly harmonious image of its many nationalities — ethnic minority folk dances performed, standardised, and interpreted through a Han lens.

This project extracts the motions of these standardised ethnic dances and reconstructs them through illustration and collage — paper cuts, constrained gestures, prescribed forms. In making visible the choreography of national identity, the work asks: whose body is dancing, and for whom?

This world is hard. Control and being controlled. Where do emotions go? When are they allowed to land?

Ninety drawings. No answer.

非典后遗症 / Pandemic

Drawing · Illustration · Beijing · Pre-RCA

SARS came when I was still small. I remember the smell of disinfectant — sharp, institutional, everywhere. Beijing won the Olympic bid that same year. The city was full of hope. I didn't know yet that this smell would follow me.

Years later I returned to Beijing to make this work. I interviewed people. I nearly fell apart.

Then COVID came, and the smell returned.

The tent: a body folded into a hidden space, privacy stripped away, sinking slowly into dark. This is how we have always lived — inside forces we didn't choose, inside systems that contain us without asking.

I am still rebuilding. But what about those who never came back? Those whose bodies were taken quietly, whose suffering disappeared from public view? They became the most private kind of grief. Swallowed. Unwitnessed. Gone.

逼婚 / Forced Marriage

Performance · Illustration · Beijing · Pre-RCA

A project born from personal rupture — the pressure to conform, to stay, to become someone else's idea of a good daughter. Made during a period of depression and self-discovery, Forced Marriage assembles the absurd collisions of individual freedom against the weight of family expectation and national belonging.

The performers are extensions of the artist — a collective body held together and pulled apart. Closeness and distance. The desire to escape and the impossibility of leaving. A frustration that does not resolve.
